# Step Pass reward rows missing after early monthly purchase

**Date:** 2026-06-02 14:21
**Files:** production data only; affected tables `personal_fitness_pass_rewards`, `cab_log_events`
**Severity:** medium

## Problem

A user paid for Step Pass Pro through the web store and the payment/order finalized successfully, but the personal Step Pass had no reward rows. The subscription entitlement was active, while the reward UI could not show the expected pass rewards.

## Root Cause

The shop payment callback finalized before the monthly standard `fitness_pass` reward rows were available. `CabShopService._ensure_personal_step_pass_rewards` copies rewards from the active standard monthly pass at finalization time. At the time of this user's callback, no source rewards were available, so the order fulfillment recorded `rewards_copied=0`; the monthly source rewards were seeded later.

## Solution

Performed a targeted, idempotent production backfill:

- Copied the active monthly `fitness_pass_rewards` rows into the affected `personal_fitness_pass_rewards`.
- Used `ON CONFLICT ON CONSTRAINT uq_personal_fitness_pass_rewards DO NOTHING`.
- Verified the personal pass remains active, resolves as tier `pro`, has two reward rows, and has no stuck paid shop payment sessions.
- Added a `cab_log_events` audit event for the manual Step Pass reward backfill.

## Prevention

- Add regression coverage for shop Step Pass finalization when standard monthly rewards are missing or seeded after purchase.
- Consider a reconciliation/backfill command for active personal Step Passes with zero reward rows after monthly rewards become available.
- During monthly Step Pass rollout, seed standard `fitness_pass` and `fitness_pass_rewards` before enabling/relying on purchasable Step Pass offers.
